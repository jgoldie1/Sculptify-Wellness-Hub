Supplier review card

Before accepting a supplier, record: legal business name, website, sample outcome, certifications verified with issuers, unit cost, freight, packaging, minimum order, production lead time, defects/returns policy, and date reviewed. “Vetted” means evidence checked—not merely listed in a directory.

Profit check

Price minus product, packaging, inbound freight, outbound shipping subsidy, payment fees, refunds and marketing cost gives estimated contribution. Fixed overhead and taxes still matter.
