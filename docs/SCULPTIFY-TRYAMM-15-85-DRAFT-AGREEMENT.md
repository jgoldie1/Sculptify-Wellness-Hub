# DRAFT — SculptifyLTD / TRYAMM Website Sales Partnership
**For discussion only. Not effective until signed by both parties.**

Parties: SculptifyLTD ("Merchant") and TRYAMM, LLC ("Platform Partner"). Confirm exact legal names, addresses, tax details and authorized signatories before signing.

## 1. Free website and ownership
Platform Partner provides the initial Sculptify website build and setup at **$0**. Merchant retains ownership of its brand, business, customer relationships, product/service catalog and merchant payment account. Hosting, domain, Shopify subscriptions, shipping, product inventory and other third-party fees require separate written allocation and approval. The website gift does not transfer TRYAMM's pre-existing platform software or licensed technology.

## 2. Scope of covered sales
Only **new website sales** originating from Sculptify's approved website checkout after the effective date and recorded in the agreed transaction ledger are covered. Existing offline sales, sales on unrelated platforms and pre-effective-date orders are excluded unless the parties agree in writing. StreetVerse San Diego referrals that complete a covered website checkout are included. Bookings or leads alone do not create a commission.

## 3. Proposed revenue split
For each eligible, fully paid, non-refunded order: **85% of Eligible Net Receipts to Sculptify and 15% to TRYAMM**.

**Eligible Net Receipts** means money actually settled from a covered sale, excluding (a) sales taxes collected and remitted, (b) shipping amounts collected and passed through to the shipping provider, (c) refunds, chargebacks and discounts, and (d) payment processing fees directly charged on the order. No negative commission; reversals adjust subsequent statements or require a documented reconciliation. Product costs, inventory, marketing, subscriptions and other overhead are not automatically deducted from this base. Parties may revise this definition only by signed amendment.

Example: a $100 order with $8 sales tax, $7 pass-through shipping and $3 payment fee produces $82 Eligible Net Receipts: Sculptify $69.70 and TRYAMM $12.30, before inventory and other costs. This is an illustration, not a processed transaction.

## 4. Merchant of record and settlement
Sculptify is the proposed merchant of record, subject to payment-provider eligibility and approval. Payments should enter Sculptify's authorized payment account; the split is calculated from verified payment-provider events and settled balances. Do not route funds through TRYAMM or activate automated split transfers until processor support, contracts, tax treatment and applicable marketplace/payment-facilitator obligations are reviewed. No self-reported order, abandoned checkout or client-side event creates payable earnings.

## 5. Reporting, timing and disputes
Provide a monthly statement listing covered order IDs, dates, settled receipts, exclusions, refunds, commission base and each party's share. Reconcile to provider records before payment. Proposed settlement: within 15 business days after month end, subject to cleared funds and chargeback reserves agreed in writing. Merchant can dispute a statement within 30 days, without waiving legal rights.

## 6. Growth and StreetVerse
TRYAMM may promote Sculptify via its approved StreetVerse San Diego listing, QR Business Passport, website links, AI tools and owner-approved campaigns. No false reviews, unapproved health claims, use of owner photographs without consent, or unapproved promotional expenses. Game listing is not represented as live until verified.

## 7. Data, security and access
Merchant controls customer-facing privacy notices and approved data uses. Each party accesses only data necessary for its obligations; no health records or card data in the game or commission ledger. Use least-privilege accounts, secure payment webhooks, logs, backups, incident notification and termination access revocation. Agree on retention, deletion and customer-data export procedures.

## 8. Term, termination and continuity
Proposed initial term: 90-day pilot from signing and go-live, renewable by written agreement. Either party may terminate on 30 days' written notice, subject to accrued obligations. On termination, Merchant retains its business, customer data export and approved website assets; access to separately licensed TRYAMM AI/platform software follows the agreed license. No commission on future sales after the termination effective date unless separately agreed.
