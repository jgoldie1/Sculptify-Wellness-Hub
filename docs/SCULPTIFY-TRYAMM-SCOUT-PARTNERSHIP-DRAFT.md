# DRAFT — Sculptify × TRYAMM Business Scout Partnership
**Proposed terms only — not active until both parties approve.**

## Ownership and independent revenue
Sculptify receives its website build and initial setup free. Sculptify retains 100% of all revenue from its own services, products, bookings, Academy and other Sculptify operations. TRYAMM has no right to deduct, intercept, or split those receipts.

## Scout opportunity
Sculptify may introduce new customers and businesses to TRYAMM through approved QR Business Passports, StreetVerse San Diego, personalized referral links, or direct referrals recorded with consent.

**Eligible sale:** A new customer/business independently purchases a designated TRYAMM service or subscription; the payment is confirmed and settled by TRYAMM's processor, is attributable to Sculptify under an agreed attribution window, and is not cancelled, refunded, fraudulent, duplicate or otherwise excluded by published program terms.

**Commission:** Percentage or fixed bounty **to Sculptify**, to be negotiated and agreed in writing. No 15/85 split or rate is assumed. Commission base (e.g. first collected subscription payment vs recurring payments), eligibility period, exclusions, refund reversals, minimum payout, payout timing, tax documentation and dispute process must be approved before activation.

## Customer safeguards
No unsolicited outreach, spam, misleading earnings claims, false testimonials, coerced signups, or commission tied to regulated patient referrals, insurance placements or healthcare decisions. Follow applicable marketing, referral, tax, privacy and professional licensing requirements. Do not expose health/financial records to StreetVerse.

## Implementation
- Separate ledgers: Sculptify-owned sales (not subject to TRYAMM commissions) versus TRYAMM Scout referrals (eligible for commissions paid by TRYAMM).
- Referral record: scout ID, customer consent, source, event timestamp, referral token, eligible TRYAMM product, processor-verified payment ID, settled amount, approved commission rule, refund/reversal and payout status.
- No payout without verified payment and approved rule. A referral click or lead is not a completed sale.
- StreetVerse San Diego may display Sculptify and its Business QR Passport only after owner approval and live integration tests.
- Both parties approve branding, data access, business terms and Scout program before launch.
