# Sculptify Full Business-in-a-Box — Operations & Funding Acceptance Plan

## Operating principles
Gift the standalone Sculptify website/app to the owner. Initial hosting stays on the sponsor's existing Vercel network, with migration/export support and independent owner permissions. Do not make business decisions, financial attestations, grant submissions or payments without owner approval. Do not claim unverified certifications, revenue or business facts.

## E-commerce
- Owner can edit product listings, variants, images, stock/fulfillment method, pricing, tax/shipping/returns disclosures and availability.
- Product Scout: weekly Wednesday research (up to five lower-risk accessories/kits/merchandise) with source, quoted cost, retail test range, sample/MOQ, shipping, return policy, branding, risks and owner decision.
- Orders: cart, live Stripe Checkout after activation, webhook verification, fulfillment status, refunds, and margin reporting. Never mark an order paid based solely on redirect.
- Lead and customer inquiries use tenant-scoped access controls, no sensitive data in AI prompts.

## Daily AI business assistance
- Quantum SEO Daily: public indexability, technical checks, San Diego local intent, meaningful content, reviews, legitimate backlinks, competitor changes, top three priorities and KPI next steps. When domain is unavailable, pre-launch readiness only.
- HoloGPT concierge uses owner-approved service catalog, policies and course facts; no unsupported medical advice or invented credentials.
- Owner coach produces prioritized daily tasks; human approval required for publishing, spending and customer communications.

## Funding Center
- First business day monthly: verify official opportunities in San Diego/California, women-owned business programs where ownership eligibility is verified, SBA, contracts, corporate/foundation grants, accelerators and supplier diversity.
- Clearly classify grant vs loan vs contract vs prize vs credit vs certification vs accelerator.
- Record source URL, program name, award/terms, verified eligibility, deadline, evidence, documents, application state, next action.
- Draft applications using only verified facts. Require explicit owner approval before legal attestations, submission, financial disclosure or borrowing.

## Staffing and Academy
- Course listings show verified instructor qualifications, prerequisites, actual schedule and policies.
- Provider applications require owner review; no public credential claims before verification.
- Employer leads and student inquiries tracked independently.

## Resilience and owner handoff
- Friday GitHub CI/dependency alerts, Supabase RLS/Edge Functions, secrets, backups and restore check.
- Owner-controlled branding and photo editing, save/preview/publish/rollback; accessible mobile PWA.
- Export business settings, catalog, approved assets and customer data through authorized secure methods for migration.

## Release gates
- Test actual owner authentication and all public forms.
- Confirm live payments, refunds, shipping, taxes, and policies before launch.
- Validate SEO canonical/sitemap/robots/schema on final domain.
- No paid services or inventory purchased without authorization.
