# SculptifyLTD x TRYAMM x StreetVerse San Diego — Partnership Specification

**Status:** Proposed partnership terms. No commission agreement, live payment split, game integration or verified transaction routing has been activated.

## Partnership
- Sculptify's website build and initial setup are gifted at $0 to its owner.
- The owner retains ownership and control of the Sculptify business, branding, domain and customer relationships, subject to mutually agreed terms.
- TRYAMM/Stubbs AI earns a mutually agreed percentage only of eligible, attributable, verified sales specified in a written agreement. No rate is assumed.
- Define whether the agreement covers marketplace products, StreetVerse-referred purchases, new online sales, or other agreed categories. Exclude existing unrelated sales unless explicitly agreed.
- Determine the commission base: gross receipts or net of tax, shipping, refunds, discounts, chargebacks, processor fees and product costs; write down which items are excluded.
- Keep a transaction ledger: sale ID, source/referral attribution, merchant, sale status, settlement, commission calculation, refunds/reversals and payout status. Never credit commission on an unverified or unpaid order.
- Merchant-of-record, Stripe/Shopify account ownership, tax responsibilities, refund policy and payout authorization require written agreement and secure payment-provider configuration. No guaranteed income.

## StreetVerse San Diego
- Create a discoverable Sculptify location/business listing in the San Diego game district, with approved branding and owner-controlled public information.
- Link the location to Sculptify's public website and booking/store entry points; use a Business QR Passport with source attribution and consent-aware analytics.
- Proposed missions: discover Sculptify, learn about wellness services, explore the Academy, book a consultation, and shop approved products.
- Game rewards and promotions require explicit budgets and safeguards. Never tie rewards to fake reviews or deceptive testimonials.
- Verify the StreetVerse runtime integration before claiming the location is live or playable.

## Growth program
- First 30 days: owner-approved brand/photos, booking test, product catalog vetting, baseline conversion metrics, legal terms and transparent commission agreement.
- Days 31–60: launch approved content, local promotions, QR attribution, customer follow-up and product/supplier samples.
- Days 61–90: analyze traffic, completed sales, refunds, contribution margin, repeat bookings and partner earnings; optimize based on evidence.

## Security and non-regression
- Separate business accounts and least-privilege access; owner can export business data.
- Do not expose personal medical records or payment details in game analytics.
- All financial events verified server-side and reconciled to payment provider before payouts.
- Preview, test, and approve changes before deployment. Keep production rollback and encrypted backups.
