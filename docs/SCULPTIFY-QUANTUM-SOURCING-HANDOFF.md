# Sculptify Quantum Sourcing — Owner-Ready Handoff

## Gift and ownership
Deliver Sculptify as a standalone branded website and editable PWA. Do not require Shopify activation, supplier purchase, inventory commitments, or billing before the owner receives the site. Owner may activate integrations later. Initial hosting on sponsor's Vercel project; migration documentation and independent owner access required.

## Revenue lanes
1. Massage therapy and body-sculpting booking, memberships and gift cards subject to verified credentials, local rules, and treatment consent.
2. Sculptify branded products: wellness accessories, body care and kits; launch only with verified supplier, product safety/labeling and claims.
3. Academy workshops/courses and student kits subject to appropriate training approvals and honest credential language.

## Quantum Sourcing scorecard (decision support, not a supplier certification)
Each candidate: supplier legal identity, website, business contact, manufacturer/authorized distributor status, product specification, sample result, certificates checked against issuer, MOQ, unit cost, setup fees, freight, duties, packaging, lead time, stock, shipping origin, return and defect terms, insurance/recall risk, independent references, and date checked. Status: unreviewed / evidence received / sample tested / owner approved / rejected. Never label 'vetted' without completed checks and evidence.

## Profit calculator
Landed unit cost = unit price + inbound freight + duties + packaging + labeling + other landed fees.
Contribution per unit = selling price - landed unit cost - payment fees - outbound shipping subsidy - expected refunds/returns - variable acquisition cost.
Contribution margin = contribution / selling price. Exclude fixed overhead from contribution, but show it separately for operating profit. Model best/base/worst scenarios; never promise huge profits.

## Owner Studio workflow
Browse candidate products -> compare suppliers -> request sample -> review claims/compliance -> approve SKU -> edit product copy/images/price -> preview -> publish to Shopify when owner authorizes connection -> receive verified order -> fulfill and track -> reconcile margin.
Buttons may be displayed as future features only if not yet wired; never show demo controls as functional commerce.

## Starter pack deliverables
Brand style and logo usage, founder photo slots, editable product card templates, approved product catalog draft, supplier vetting spreadsheet, margin worksheet, packaging specifications, shipping/returns policy templates, customer thank-you insert, gift card and social content templates, simple handoff guide.

## Risk controls
Cosmetic and topical products require ingredient/label and applicable regulatory review; body-sculpting devices may be regulated. Massage licensure and local business requirements must be verified. Protect client information; no unapproved medical claims or testimonials.
