**Candidate:** Raymond Jarreau
**Status:** Proposed; aware of the founder's broader work, not formally offered or accepted.
**Proposed title:** Operations Director, OmniCare 360
**Sponsor:** TRYAMM / Stubbs AI founder

## Mission
Develop OmniCare 360 as a coordinated service-navigation and wellness partner network, with responsible referrals, clear customer communications, verified providers, and approved AI assistance. Do not present the service as a licensed healthcare provider, insurer, pharmacy or legal practice unless duly authorized.

## Proposed responsibilities
- Map service lines and approved providers, maintain qualification/credential review.
- Set up intake, consent, referral follow-up, complaints and incident escalation.
- Coordinate operations and partner relationships; monitor cost, service quality and customer satisfaction.
- Review insurance and retirement-related partnerships only within verified licenses and registrations.
- Work with founder to prioritize product requirements for Stubbs AI / HoloGPT; AI does not diagnose, prescribe, quote coverage or give individualized legal/investment advice.

## Authority boundaries
Founder retains company ownership, final budget and contract approvals, IP, domain, payment accounts, and technical admin. Candidate receives least-privilege operations access only after acceptance, identity check, written agreement and privacy/security onboarding. No access to Sculptify, other vendor data or protected health/financial records by default.

## Compensation discussion (not an offer)
Choose one after scope and budget review: fixed part-time stipend, milestone-based project fee, or salary/contract arrangement. Any revenue share must be written, based on defined collected net revenue or other clear base, subject to legal review and regulatory limits. Do not promise commissions tied to regulated insurance or patient referrals without legal approval.

## First 90 days
Days 1–30: confirm licensing/legal boundaries, business model, service map, vendor due diligence, privacy and consent.
Days 31–60: test intake and routing with approved non-sensitive scenarios, provider agreements, customer support and quality controls.
Days 61–90: evaluate performance, costs, complaint handling, verified partner activity and readiness to expand.

## Before sending a formal offer
Confirm candidate's exact licenses/registrations, jurisdiction, availability, desired compensation, employment classification, conflicts of interest, privacy obligations, and whether the candidate accepts the proposed responsibilities. Prepare a formal agreement after mutual review.
