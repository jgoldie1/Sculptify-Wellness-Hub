# TRYAMM / All American Marketplace — 15 Vendor Pilot

This is a proposed pilot plan, not a deployment claim.

## Principles
- One reusable website engine and shared template components; 15 separate tenant configurations and branding sets.
- Vendors own their content, customer relationships and commerce accounts; support migration/export.
- Shared discovery via TRYAMM marketplace directory, optional Stubbs AI/HoloGPT services, analytics, and Quantum Sourcing.
- Strict tenant isolation and row-level access controls; no cross-vendor leakage of leads, orders, assets, or payment information.
- Independent owner login and domain/hosting transfer plan.

## Suggested launch cohorts
1. First 3: SculptifyLTD, La Teasia Beauty House, and one verified service vendor.
2. Next 5: expand across services and products only after testing sign-in, publishing, booking/checkout, notifications and backups.
3. Final 7: complete the pilot after operational metrics and support capacity meet launch criteria.

## Standard site template
Homepage, owner story/photo with permission, catalog/services, booking or checkout, contact, privacy/returns policies, owner editor, mobile PWA assets, SEO, marketplace profile, support and analytics.

## Integrations
- Shopify per vendor only if chosen/authorized; do not force a shared Shopify account.
- Server-verified payments, order/booking status, fulfillment and refunds.
- AI access governed by approved business knowledge and per-vendor cost budgets.
- Marketplace referral/commission rules require explicit agreement, disclosure and auditable ledger.

## Gates
Owner-approved content and licensing, working CTA buttons, mobile QA, payments test, privacy/security review, backups and restore, domain access, billing limits, handoff instructions.

## Cost controls
No new paid subscriptions or purchases without owner approval; consolidate templates, automate deployments, set alerts, avoid duplicative services and separate vendor billing.

## Launch metrics
Number of working public sites, vendor owner logins verified, completed orders/bookings, inquiry-to-sale conversion, fulfillment success, refunds, uptime, monthly cost per vendor, support tickets.
