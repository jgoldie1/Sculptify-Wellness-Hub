# SculptifyLTD × StreetVerse San Diego

## Purpose
SculptifyLTD is a StreetVerse San Diego showcase business.

The connection is designed to create a real flywheel:

Sculptify customer / student / template client
→ discovers StreetVerse
→ joins through a transparent Business QR Passport or referral link
→ becomes a verified active StreetVerse user
→ generates eligible StreetVerse platform activity
→ StreetVerse records attribution server-side
→ Sculptify may earn its disclosed partner share
→ Sculptify keeps promoting StreetVerse because the relationship is valuable

## Sculptify Partner Identity
- Business ID: `sculptifyltd-san-diego`

1. verified attribution
2. eligible referred user
3. real eligible platform activity
4. server-verified platform revenue
5. active partner terms
