# SculptifyLTD AI Business Operating System

## Core stack
- Public business app/PWA
- Supabase database + Auth + RLS
- HoloGPT — customer and owner assistant
- Stubbs AI — permanent AI/automation brand
- Gemini — optional underlying model provider
- Stripe — payment collection
- GitHub — source control, rollback, security CI
- Quantum SEO Daily — organic growth workflow
- Grant Center — funding pipeline
- Product Pipeline — sourcing workflow
- Business Academy — owner education
- Owner Coach — daily prioritized business plan

## AI jobs
HoloGPT/Stubbs AI can:
- answer approved customer FAQs
- route booking/service questions
- explain courses/store/staffing
- prepare owner daily priorities
- prepare grant drafts
- help research product candidates
- prepare SEO improvements
- draft ads, landing pages and follow-ups
- teach Business Academy modules
- identify missing launch items
- help document SOPs

AI does not independently:
- change bank/payout accounts
- buy domains
- sign supplier agreements
- certify grant/legal statements
- submit ownership/tax attestations
- publish unverified regulated products
- silently rewrite production code

## No-regression principle
Every material code change is branch → test → security scan → review → merge → verify.
