## Buyer onboarding
1. Collect approved brand name, logo, contact details, service area, services, prices and legal claims.
2. Create a separate deployment and database for each customer; do not reuse Sculptify owner credentials or customer data.
3. Configure domain, Supabase public key, RLS and allowed auth redirects.
4. Set payment links in the buyer's own payment account; never collect secret keys in client-side code.
5. Replace all Sculptify names, San Diego details and photos unless separately licensed.
6. Test mobile navigation, form delivery, admin authentication, AI, checkout, accessibility and PWA.
7. Obtain written owner signoff before publishing.
