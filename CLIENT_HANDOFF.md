# Client Template Handoff

## Owner workflow
1. Open Sculptify Owner Studio.
2. Open Template Studio.
3. Choose a template.
4. Enter the client business name.
5. Enter the client email.
6. Create the client business.
7. Send the client the `client-editor.html` link.
8. Tell the client to sign in with the exact assigned email.
9. The client edits their site, saves a draft, previews it, and publishes it.
10. Add a custom domain later if purchased.

## Client workflow
1. Open the Template Editor.
2. Enter your assigned email.
3. Open the secure magic link.
4. Edit:
   - business name
   - business type
   - location
   - phone/email
   - logo URL
   - headline/subtitle
   - colors
   - booking/store links
   - services
   - FAQs
   - products
5. Tap Save Draft.
6. Tap Preview.
7. Tap Publish.

## Security
A client can only edit sites assigned to their verified email.
Sculptify admin can manage all client sites.
Published sites are public; drafts are not publicly readable unless the signed-in editor/admin has access.

## Custom domains
The first publish uses the Sculptify-hosted client URL.
Custom-domain mapping is a separate host/DNS step and should be done after the client approves the site.
