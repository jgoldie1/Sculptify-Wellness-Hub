# SculptifyLTD Template Business Model

## What is being sold
A customizable business starter website/PWA powered by the Sculptify template system.

Each client can receive:
- a professional template
- business name, logo and color customization
- services
- FAQs
- store/products
- booking/store links
- contact information
- a secure client editor
- preview and publish controls
- optional HoloGPT / Stubbs AI assistance
- optional hosting/domain support
- optional marketing/SEO support
- optional business education and launch coaching

## Suggested ways to package it
Do not hard-code prices until the owner chooses them.

### Setup package
One-time setup:
- choose template
- add branding
- add services/products
- connect booking/payment links
- publish
- handoff training

### Monthly support
Recurring service:
- hosting/support
- minor content edits
- HoloGPT knowledge updates
- Quantum SEO reports
- security checks
- backup reminders
- analytics/revenue review

### Growth package
Optional:
- local SEO
- ad campaign preparation/management
- grant/funding research
- product sourcing
- email/SMS follow-up
- staff/provider onboarding
- content calendar

### Education package
Optional:
- owner Business Academy
- client-service SOPs
- product-sourcing SOPs
- grant-readiness SOPs
- cybersecurity basics
- business-launch checklist

## Licensing vs franchise
Selling or licensing a website/business-system template is different from creating a legal franchise. If the business later licenses the Sculptify trademark, charges fees, and materially controls how operators run a business under that mark, get appropriate franchise/legal advice before calling the arrangement a franchise.

## Client promise
A good promise is:
"Choose your template, customize your business, save, preview and publish."

Avoid promises of guaranteed revenue, guaranteed grants, guaranteed rankings, automatic licensing, or guaranteed business success.
