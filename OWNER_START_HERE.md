# SculptifyLTD Owner Start Here

## The business in one sentence
SculptifyLTD is a San Diego wellness platform with services, Body Sculpting education, provider/staffing pathways, a store, booking, HoloGPT and Stubbs AI.

## What the owner controls
The owner controls:
- public business name, phone, email and website URL
- services and approved prices
- course information
- product approval and store checkout links
- provider/student applications
- grant/application approval
- payment and payout accounts
- supplier agreements
- advertising budgets
- final legal/certification statements

AI can assist with research, drafting, education and operations, but it must not invent business facts or legally certify them.

## Every day
1. Open Owner Studio.
2. Tap **Ask HoloGPT: What Should I Do Today?**
3. Review new bookings and customer questions.
4. Check HoloGPT questions that need a better approved FAQ.
5. Complete the highest-priority task.
6. Review the Quantum SEO daily action.

## Every week
1. Review booked revenue vs ad spend.
2. Review the product sourcing pipeline.
3. Review new grant/funding candidates.
4. Review security alerts and admin access.
5. Update one FAQ/service/course if customers are repeatedly confused.
6. Verify upcoming appointments and class commitments.

## Every month
1. Download a private business backup.
2. Reconcile Stripe payouts, refunds, fees and advertising costs.
3. Complete one Business Academy module.
4. Review product margins and supplier performance.
5. Review grant documents and certification readiness.
6. Review organic SEO and legitimate backlinks.
7. Review customer retention and repeat-booking opportunities.

## First 30 days
### Week 1 — Launch foundation
- Choose final domain.
- Deploy the standalone repository.
- Configure HTTPS.
- Add the final domain to Supabase Auth redirects.
- Test owner login.
- Create public business phone and business email.
- Save the final website URL in Owner Studio.

### Week 2 — Money and offers
- Connect Stripe.
- Create payment/checkout links.
- Publish only verified services and pricing.
- Build one simple new-customer offer.
- Set clear refund/cancellation policies.
- Test the full booking-to-payment flow.

### Week 3 — Store and Academy
- Choose low-risk supplier products.
- Order samples when practical.
- Calculate landed cost and margin.
- Publish only approved products.
- Finalize Body Sculpting course outcomes, prerequisites, practical requirements and refund policy.
- Verify instructor qualifications and what the certificate legally represents.

### Week 4 — Growth
- Launch local Google Search first.
- Add Meta/TikTok creative testing.
- Turn on Quantum SEO Daily.
- Request legitimate reviews from satisfied customers.
- Build local landing pages only when they contain useful, non-duplicative content.
- Start the monthly grant/funding pipeline.

## Money rules
- Never spend more on ads because clicks look impressive; scale only from booked customers and revenue.
- Never publish supplier products before checking margin, shipping, returns and quality.
- Never expose Stripe secrets in the browser or public repo.
- Keep payout-account changes under owner control.
- Separate business and personal records as the business matures.

## Customer-service rules
- Confirm appointments.
- Keep intake appropriate to the service.
- Explain what the service can and cannot do.
- Avoid unsupported medical claims.
- Follow up after service.
- Ask for feedback/reviews with consent.
- Offer packages when they genuinely fit the client.

## Academy rules
- Clearly state what a course teaches.
- Clearly state what a certificate does and does not authorize.
- Keep attendance/completion records.
- Verify practical training requirements.
- Publish prerequisites and refund terms.
- Never call a private certificate a state/government license unless it actually is one.

## Staffing rules
- Verify provider qualifications before publishing.
- Get consent before sharing provider profiles.
- Protect applicant information.
- Keep job advertising separate from consumer demographic campaigns.
- Define whether Sculptify is acting as a recruiter, referral platform or employer.

## Store rules
Start with:
- branded merchandise
- spa accessories
- student kits
- gift cards
- service packages
- low-risk wellness accessories

Use extra review before selling:
- cosmetics
- ingestibles
- electrical devices
- body-sculpting equipment
- products making medical claims

## Cybersecurity rules
- Turn on MFA for owner Google, GitHub, Supabase, Stripe, host and domain registrar.
- Use a password manager.
- Never paste secret keys into chat or screenshots.
- Review admins after suspicious activity.
- Rotate exposed keys immediately.
- Download private backups monthly.
- Use Git history to roll back bad code changes.

## Grant rules
- Search grants, contracts, prizes, accelerators, tax credits, WOSB/WBE and supplier-diversity programs.
- Verify eligibility before spending time on a full application.
- Keep reusable documents current.
- Let HoloGPT prepare drafts.
- Owner reviews and approves.
- Submit only on the official source.
- Track won/lost outcomes and learn from them.

## When to call a human professional
Use appropriate legal, tax, insurance, licensing or compliance professionals for decisions that require professional judgment. HoloGPT can prepare questions and organize information, but should not impersonate licensed counsel, accountants or medical professionals.
